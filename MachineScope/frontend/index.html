Evaluation methodology
    Quick prediction

    Score a set of sensor readings

    Readings are sent to the Python backend, passed through the exact training-time pipeline, and scored by the Random Forest.

    Product quality variant

    No prediction yet

    Enter readings and press Predict. Results are model estimates, not confirmation of a physical failure.

    Anomaly detection

    Unusual sensor readings (IQR method)

    A reading is flagged when it falls outside [Q1 − k·IQR, Q3 + k·IQR] for its sensor. An unusual reading is not automatically a machine failure.

    —

    Maintenance planner

    Review queue

    Records that deserve a closer look, ranked by transparent rules. Priorities are not calibrated failure probabilities.
